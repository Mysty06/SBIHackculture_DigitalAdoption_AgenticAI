# SBIHackculture_DigitalAdoption_AgenticAI
# 🎓 YONO Sensei
### An Agentic AI-Powered Financial Mentor for Personalized Banking & Digital Adoption

> Transforming banking from a transactional activity into an intelligent, gamified financial growth journey.

---

## 🚀 Overview

YONO Sensei is an Agentic AI-powered digital adoption platform designed for the SBI YONO ecosystem.

The platform acts as a personalized financial mentor that learns from a customer's transaction history, banking habits, account type, and financial goals to create customized learning paths, habit-building challenges, financial simulations, and digital banking missions.

By combining Artificial Intelligence, Gamification, Financial Literacy, and Behavioral Coaching, YONO Sensei helps users confidently adopt SBI's digital services while improving their financial well-being.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Modern banking applications offer numerous services such as:

- UPI
- AutoPay
- Fixed Deposits
- Recurring Deposits
- Investments
- Insurance
- Loans
- YONO Cash

However, a significant percentage of customers only use a small subset of these services due to:

- Lack of awareness
- Low financial literacy
- Complex user journeys
- Fear of making financial mistakes
- Limited personalization

As a result, digital adoption remains low despite feature-rich platforms.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

YONO Sensei introduces an Agentic AI layer inside YONO that:

- Understands customer behavior.
- Learns from transaction history.
- Identifies financial maturity levels.
- Creates personalized financial growth journeys.
- Encourages digital adoption through gamification.
- Rewards users for learning and improving financial habits.

The AI proactively guides users instead of waiting for them to ask questions.
